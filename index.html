About Knock-Knock App

👩‍💻 v2.3 Build 008

Designed for field service territory management. Knock-Knock allows publishers to tap house roofs on high-resolution satellite imagery, log visits with exact date/timestamps, and auto-sync records across devices.

• Roof-Tap Pinning: Instant marker creation.

• Offline-First: Works without cell connectivity.

• Real-Time Sync: Built for multi-publisher care.
